\section*{Extremal Minimal Witness Lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Extremal Minimal Witness]
Assume $G \in \mathcal{Q}$ and $|V(G)| > 12$.
Suppose every certified occurrence $H \subset G$ becomes non-admissible (its reduction creates a 2-vertex cut).
Then there exists a certified occurrence $H^*$ whose pullback set $K^*$ is minimal by cardinality among all such witnesses.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
\textbf{(A) Pullback association.}
For each non-admissible certified occurrence $H \subset G$, its reduction produces a graph $G'$ with a 2-vertex cut.
Applying the Generic 2-Vertex Cut Pullback Lemma to this reduction, we obtain a nonempty set
$K_H \subseteq V(G)\setminus V(H)$ and vertices $q_H \in V(G)\setminus V(H)$ and $p_H \in \{x,y\}$
(specific to the gadget used for $H$) such that
\[
N_G(K_H) \subseteq \{q_H\} \cup B_{p_H},
\]
where $B_{p_H}$ is the terminal pair in $B$ adjacent to $p_H$ in the gadget.

\medskip
\textbf{(B) Minimal choice.}
Since $G$ is finite and, by assumption, there exists at least one non-admissible certified occurrence,
the collection $\{K_H\}$ is a finite nonempty family of finite sets.
Hence there exists a choice minimizing cardinality.
Define $K^*$ to be such a minimizer, i.e.
\[
|K^*| = \min_H |K_H|.
\]
Let $H^*$ be a certified occurrence whose pullback witness set is $K^*$.
\end{proof}
